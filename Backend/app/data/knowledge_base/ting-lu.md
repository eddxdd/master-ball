title: Ting-Lu — Strategy Notes
species_id: tinglu
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, dark, ground

Ting-Lu has the highest bulk of the "Loyal Three"/ruinous-beast Pokemon
introduced alongside it, with a base HP/Defense combination that lets it
switch into most physical attacks in the format without much concern, backed
by its ability Vessel of Ruin, which lowers every other Pokemon's Special
Attack stat on the field.

Common role: a defensive Stealth Rock setter and physical wall that trades
its own turns for chip damage (Stealth Rock, Whirlwind to phase out setup
sweepers) rather than trying to win fights outright — it usually isn't
running a heavily offensive set, since its own damage output is secondary to
the disruption and residual damage it provides.

Checks and counters: strong special attackers actually threaten it more
than most physical ones would suggest from its bulk stats, since Ting-Lu's
Special Defense, while good, isn't as overwhelming as its physical side, and
Vessel of Ruin doesn't help Ting-Lu's own matchup against a special attacker
that's already in.

Common teammates: a special wall or resist to round out the defensive
core, and setup sweepers that benefit from Ting-Lu's Vessel of Ruin
suppressing the special attackers that would otherwise threaten them.
