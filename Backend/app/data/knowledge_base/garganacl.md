title: Garganacl — Strategy Notes
species_id: garganacl
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, rock

Garganacl is one of the most durable physical walls in the format, combining
huge natural bulk with Purifying Salt (which blocks status conditions
outright and halves damage taken from Ghost-type moves) and Salt Cure, a
signature move that chips a fixed percentage of the target's max HP every
turn it's active, similar to Leech Seed but without giving Garganacl any HP
back.

Common role: a dedicated stall/wall piece that sets up Salt Cure or Stealth
Rock, then simply refuses to die to most physical attackers while residual
damage from Salt Cure, Stealth Rock, and weather/status slowly wins games
that go long.

Checks and counters: strong special attackers are the cleanest way through it,
since its Special Defense is comparatively lower than its physical bulk.
Setup sweepers that can outlast Salt Cure's chip damage and break through
its Recover-less bulk over several turns can also wear it down eventually.

Common teammates: a special wall to cover the hole Garganacl's own bulk
doesn't, and hazard-stacking partners, since Garganacl's win condition
usually involves grinding the opponent down over a long game rather than
scoring quick knockouts itself.
