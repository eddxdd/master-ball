title: Kingambit — Strategy Notes
species_id: kingambit
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, dark, steel

Kingambit is a late-game sweeper built entirely around its ability,
Supreme Overlord, which increases its move power by roughly 10% for every
teammate that has already fainted, up to a 50% boost with a full team down.
That scaling makes it one of the best "closer" Pokemon in the format: it gets
strictly more dangerous as a long, grindy game goes on, which rewards playing
patiently around it rather than trying to force damage early.

Common role: a wallbreaker/sweeper that comes in during the mid-to-late game
once a few Pokemon on both sides are already down, using Sucker Punch to
punish slower attackers on the switch and Iron Head/Kowtow Cleave to break
through what's left. Dark/Steel typing gives it a clean resistance profile
against Psychic- and Fairy-type attackers that would otherwise threaten a
mid-game sweep.

Checks and counters: Fighting-type coverage moves are its main defensive
weakness. Faster Pokemon that can outspeed and OHKO it before it gets a
Sucker Punch off are the cleanest answer, since Sucker Punch fails entirely
against a slower target using a status move or against a faster attacker
moving first.

Common teammates: Hazard-setters and pivots that can whittle down the
opposing team's HP earlier in the game, directly feeding Supreme Overlord's
scaling, plus a Fighting-resistant teammate that can safely switch into the
moves that threaten Kingambit itself.
