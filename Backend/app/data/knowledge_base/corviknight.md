title: Corviknight — Strategy Notes
species_id: corviknight
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, flying, steel

Corviknight is one of the most reliable defensive backbones in the format:
Flying/Steel typing gives it a huge resistance list (immune to Ground, resists
Fighting, Bug, Grass, and several others) with only Fire and Electric as
common exploitable weaknesses, and its ability Pressure drains the opposing
Pokemon's PP faster, which matters over a long defensive game.

Common role: a physically-defensive pivot running Defog to clear hazards for
the whole team, U-turn for momentum, and Roost to stay healthy indefinitely
against anything that can't break through its bulk in one or two hits. It's
one of the most common hazard-removal answers in the format specifically
because so few things threaten it while it's doing that job.

Checks and counters: strong Electric- or Fire-type special attackers punish
it hardest, since its Special Defense investment is usually secondary to its
physical bulk on a standard set. Setup sweepers that don't mind Roost's
stall also eventually break through if Corviknight has no offensive
answer of its own.

Common teammates: an Electric- or Fire-resist teammate to patch the shared
weakness, and a Stealth Rock setter, since Corviknight itself usually
prioritizes Defog over hazard-setting.
