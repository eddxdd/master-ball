title: Iron Valiant — Strategy Notes
species_id: ironvaliant
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, fairy, fighting

Iron Valiant combines a very high base 136 Speed with strong dual attacking
stats (base 130 Attack and Special Attack), letting it run either a physical
or special-leaning set — or, less commonly, a genuinely mixed set — that
opponents can't always scout for safely before committing a switch-in.

Common role: a fast offensive breaker/revenge killer that comes in against a
weakened or slower target and threatens a knockout immediately, most
commonly as a Choice Specs/Scarf user or a Life Orb mixed attacker, rather
than a stall-oriented or setup piece — its fragile defenses mean it wants to
be attacking, not switching into hits.

Checks and counters: priority moves and anything that outpaces its already
high Speed threaten it before it can act, since its physical and special
bulk are both well below average. Common resists to Fairy/Fighting coverage
(Steel- and Poison-types especially) also blunt its offensive pressure if
Iron Valiant lacks the right coverage move.

Common teammates: Stealth Rock support to convert its chip damage into
cleaner knockout ranges, and a slower, bulkier teammate that can safely
switch into the priority users and faster attackers Iron Valiant itself
can't handle.
