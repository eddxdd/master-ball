title: Toxapex — Strategy Notes
species_id: toxapex
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, poison, water

Toxapex has the highest Special Defense-to-investment efficiency of any
common Water-type wall in the format, and its ability Regenerator heals a
chunk of its max HP every time it switches out — combined with Baneful Bunker
(a protection move that also poisons anything making physical contact) and
Toxic Spikes, it's built to outlast almost any single attacker across a full
game rather than win a single exchange.

Common role: a dedicated special wall and hazard-setter (Toxic Spikes) that
pivots in and out constantly, healing back most of the damage it takes via
Regenerator, while chipping the opponent's team with poison damage over time.

Checks and counters: strong Electric- or Ground-type attacks are common
answers, since neither is resisted by its Poison/Water typing. Setup
sweepers that boost Attack or Special Attack past what Toxapex's residual
poison damage can race against also eventually break through, since Toxapex
itself rarely threatens meaningful direct damage back.

Common teammates: a Ground- or Electric-resist teammate to cover its
shared weakness, and physical wallbreakers or setup sweepers that benefit
from the Toxic Spikes chip damage Toxapex lays down over the course of a
game.
