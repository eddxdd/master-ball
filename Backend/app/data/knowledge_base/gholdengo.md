title: Gholdengo — Strategy Notes
species_id: gholdengo
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, steel, ghost

Gholdengo's Steel/Ghost typing is unique in the current format and gives it
only one common weakness (Ground, and only if it lacks a way to avoid it),
while its ability Good as Gold blocks all incoming status moves entirely —
Toxic, Will-O-Wisp, Thunder Wave, and Taunt all fail against it outright. That
single ability is a huge part of its value: it lets Gholdengo switch into
would-be status-support Pokemon completely safely.

Common role: a special attacker that leans on Shadow Ball and Make It Rain
for coverage that's rarely resisted cleanly, often running Recover to turn
itself into a genuinely hard-to-remove long-term answer to a wide swath of
special attackers rather than a one-time check.

Checks and counters: strong physical attackers that don't rely on status to
function, and anything that can outright OHKO it with a super-effective or
strong neutral physical hit before Gholdengo can threaten back, since its
own bulk is only middling on the physical side.

Common teammates: physically-defensive pivots that cover the physical
attackers Gholdengo struggles with, and hazard-removal support, since
Gholdengo itself doesn't clear hazards and appreciates switching in cleanly
to use Recover.
