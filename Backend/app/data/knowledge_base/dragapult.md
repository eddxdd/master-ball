title: Dragapult — Strategy Notes
species_id: dragapult
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, dragon, ghost

Dragapult's base 142 Speed is the fastest of any fully-evolved Dragon-type in
the format not relying on a stat boost, which lets it comfortably outrun the
vast majority of the unboosted metagame while still hitting hard from either
attacking stat. Its Dragon/Ghost typing is offensively excellent (very few
common answers resist both) but defensively fragile — it does not want to
take hits.

Common role: a revenge killer and hit-and-run attacker that comes in on a
weakened target, picks off something with a strong neutral or
super-effective hit, and uses U-turn to keep momentum on the side that's
ahead, rather than staying in and tanking a return hit.

Checks and counters: strong priority moves and faster Choice Scarf users
punish its low physical bulk before it gets to move. Fairy- and Steel-type
resistances to both of its main STAB types cut its offensive pressure down to
whatever coverage it's carrying.

Common teammates: Stealth Rock support to convert its chip damage into
knockouts, and a dedicated special wall or Steel-type that can absorb the
priority/Scarf-user matchups Dragapult itself can't take.
