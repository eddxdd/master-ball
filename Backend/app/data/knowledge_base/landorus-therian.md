title: Landorus-Therian — Strategy Notes
species_id: landorustherian
tags: pokemon_strategy, OU, ground, flying

Landorus-Therian is one of the most reliable defensive Ground-types in singles
formats, built around its Intimidate ability and a strong Ground/Flying
defensive typing that resists both Ground and Fighting-type coverage aimed at
its checks. Its base 145 Attack lets it threaten real damage even from a
support-leaning set, so opponents can't just ignore it as a pure Intimidate
battery.

Common role: a pivot that repeatedly weakens incoming physical attackers via
Intimidate while chipping the field with Stealth Rock and U-turn, then
switching out to preserve momentum. Its access to U-turn specifically lets it
apply Intimidate's damage-reduction, gain tempo, and bring in a teammate that
wants the resulting favorable matchup, all in one move.

Checks and counters: Ice-type attackers (Landorus-Therian's Flying typing is
weak to Ice) and other Ground-immune Flying/Levitate Pokemon that shrug off
its Earthquake are the cleanest answers. Because it has no reliable way to
touch Corviknight or other bulky Flying-types offensively without a coverage
move, teams built around Landorus-Therian usually pair it with a teammate that
can pressure those switch-ins directly.

Common teammates: Water- or Steel-type partners that appreciate the Ground/
Fire/Rock coverage gaps Intimidate support opens up, and setup sweepers that
want the extra turn Intimidate's Attack drop buys against an opposing
physical attacker.
